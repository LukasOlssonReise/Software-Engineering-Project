## Sprint 2 Goal

- **Project Name:** Campus Exchange
- **Customer's Name:** Henry Edison
- **Sprint Goal:** 
	After this sprint students will be able to filter items by category to quickly find what they are looking for. They will be able to view their personal information and update them on their account page. Students will also be able to lend out their items that they are currently not using, they will be findable in the browse page for other students.
