## Sprint 3 Goal

- **Project Name:**
	- Campus Exchange
- **Customer's Name:**
	- Henry Edison
- **Sprint Goal:** 
    - With this sprint I want students to be able to offer their items from a protected personal page, other students can request to borrow them for a chosen period. The owner of the item will get a request and can accept or reject, they can also mark items as returned so they can be offered again.
