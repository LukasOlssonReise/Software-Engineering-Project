## **Spring Goal**

**Project Name:** Campus Exchange
**Customer Name:** Henry Edison
**Sprint Goal:**
Students will be able to borrow items from other students and be able to lend out their own items. Students with the owned items will be notified when someone wants to borrow one of their items, then they will be able to accept or reject the request.
